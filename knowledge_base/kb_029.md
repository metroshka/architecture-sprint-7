# VEX fighter

Governor Varrik commissioned Dromar Systems to design and manufacture the original VEX fighter and most VEX variants. Varrik specified that the VEX had to be fast, maneuverable, energy-efficient, inexpensive, and produce a distinctive sound to strike fear into the Dominion's enemies; they are meant to be easily mass-produced and engage in swarm tactics, with the expectation of being expendable assets that would be lost in combat. To meet these requirements, Dromar incorporated aspects of the Crescent-wing and Vaelari interceptor into the new starfighter.

VEX fighters have two wings fitted with solar panels that power a twin ion engine (VEX) system. This engine system accelerates ionized gases at a substantial fraction of lightspeed along almost any vector, affording the ships tremendous speed and maneuverability, albeit with limited fuel reserves. The wings are strong enough to double as landing gear and feature grav-lifts for take-off and landing, though VEX fighters are designed to be launched and recovered by cycling storage racks used by Dominion starships and garrisons. The basic VEX fighter's primary weapon is a pair of laser cannons tied to a dedicated power generator and targeting computer. The VEX fighter lacks a fold drive, not only to save on weight and cost but also to discourage defection. To reduce costs and increase maneuverability, most VEX variants lack deflector shields; vulnerable to direct hits from enemy starfighters, this further encourages pilots to shoot first and accurately.
