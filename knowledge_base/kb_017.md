# Velisar

Velisar is a politically and strategically important planet, serving as the capital and seat of government for the Commonwealth and the Vastran Dominion, as well as the headquarters of the Vaelari Order. It is a bustling, yet highly stratified planet-spanning metropolis. Throughout the city's centuries-long development, new city blocks were built on top of old ones, forming levels. Velisar has 5127 levels, with the top being the wealthiest and the lowest being the poorest.

Velisar has four moons and is the sixth planet out of the eleven that make up the system of the same name. It lies within the Velisar Subsector of the Velis Sector, located in the Inner Reach galactic quadrant region. The sun, Velisar Prime, is the zero coordinate of the galaxy (as opposed to being its galactic center).
