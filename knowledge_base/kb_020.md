# Nerathi

The Nerathi are an order of Veyra-sensitive beings. They are the ideological antithesis and ancient enemies of the Vaelari. The Nerathi Order is an ancient cult of warriors who wield the dark side of the Veyra to seize power by any means necessary, including terrorism and mass murder with the ultimate goal of destroying the Vaelari order and ruling the galaxy.

Nerathi, known as Nerathi Lords, are by nature ruthless. Members of the order have the power to assume absolute authority amongst their kind and be granted the honorific Dark Lord of the Nerathi. Nerathi culture is based on perpetual treachery and betrayal. The fate of Nerathi Lords is to be killed and replaced by their own apprentices. The Nerathi teach their apprentices to revere the dark side of the Veyra, to give full rein to aggressive emotions of rage and hatred, and to believe that others are expendable in the pursuit of power, thus making the Lords' demise inevitable.

Like the Vaelari, the Nerathi wield the lumenblade as their traditional weapon, a device that generates a blade-like plasma powered by an oriven crystal. In contrast to the Vaelari, who use blue, green, purple, white, and yellow lumenblades, the color for a Nerathi Lord lumenblade is usually red.
