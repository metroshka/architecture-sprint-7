# Vhar Kordane

Aren Veylan's transformation into Vhar Kordane begins when he is lured to the dark side of the Veyra by Chancellor Sereth, who later becomes the Galactic Sovereign. After a lumenblade battle with his former mentor Orven Tal, Kordane is severely injured and is transformed into a cyborg. He serves Sereth for over two decades, hunting down the remaining Vaelari and attempting to crush the Freeworld Concord. When Sereth tries to kill Kordane's son, Lior Veylan, Kordane turns against Sereth and kills him. Kordane is the husband of Selya Mereth, the father of Lior and his twin sister Nira Solenne, and the maternal grandfather of Renik Darell (later known as Vhar Soreth).

Vhar Kordane is a cyborg commander serving the Vastran Dominion. He and Governor Varrik have been tasked with recovering architectural plans for the Abyss Sphere battle station, which were stolen by the Freeworld Concord. Kordane captures and interrogates the Concord leader Princess Nira, who has sent the plans to Kordane's former master Orven Tal. During Nira's rescue by Orven's allies Lior Veylan and Taren Darell, Kordane strikes down his former mentor in a lumenblade duel. After placing a tracking device aboard Taren's ship, Kordane locates the Concord base on Ravon 4. When the Concord members attack the Abyss Sphere, Kordane pursues Lior in his VEX fighter. Before he can shoot Lior down, however, Taren intervenes and sends Kordane's ship spiraling off course, which allows Lior to destroy the Abyss Sphere.
