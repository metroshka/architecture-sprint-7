# Orven Tal

Orven Tal is an elderly hermit living on Sovar. When Lior and V7-N6 travel the desert in search of the runaway automaton K8-M4, Orven rescues them from Dune Wardens. At Orven's home, K8-M4 plays a recording of Princess Nira explaining that K8-M4 contains architectural plans for the Vastran Dominion's planet-destroying battle station, the Abyss Sphere. Nira asks Orven to deliver K8-M4 and the plans to Elaris to aid the Freeworld Concord. Orven reveals to Lior that he is a Vaelari, a peacekeeper from the days of the Commonwealth. He explains that Lior's father was also a Vaelari, and was killed by Vhar Kordane. Orven gives Lior his father's lumenblade and invites him to come to Elaris and begin Vaelari training. At first Lior declines, but changes his mind after finding that his aunt and uncle have been killed by Dominion armored legionaries.

As Orven and Lior approach Namar Port, Orven uses a Vaelari mind trick to compel Dominion troops to let them through a checkpoint. Orven and Lior hire the smugglers Taren Darell and Ruvakka to take them to Elaris aboard Taren's ship, the Vesper Kite. During the journey, Orven instructs Lior in lumenblade combat. Orven and the others find that the Dominion has obliterated Elaris. The Kite gets caught in the Abyss Sphere's capture beam, but the group is able to avoid detection and infiltrate the station. Orven disables the capture beam, then encounters Kordane. They engage in a lumenblade duel as Lior, Nira, Taren and Ruvakka escape. Orven allows Kordane to strike him down, then vanishes into the Veyra. Later, Orven contacts Lior through the Veyra and helps him destroy the Abyss Sphere.
