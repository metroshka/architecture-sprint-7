# Vesper Kite

The Vesper Kite, an LF-2400 Velari light freighter, is primarily commanded by smuggler Taren Darell and his Ruunak first mate, Ruvakka, and was previously owned by gambler/con-artist Velin Ardos.

Taren Darell won the Vesper Kite from Velin Ardos in the card game triad meld.

Orven Tal and Lior Veylan charter the ship in the Namar Port cantina to deliver them, V7-N6, K8-M4, and the stolen Abyss Sphere plans to Elaris. When the Kite is captured by the Abyss Sphere, the group conceal themselves in smuggling compartments built into the floor to avoid detection during a search of the ship. The group eventually escapes aboard the Kite after rescuing Princess Nira. Darell later collects his fee for delivering them to the hidden Concord base on Ravon 4 and departs under bitter circumstances, but returns to assist Lior in destroying the Abyss Sphere.
