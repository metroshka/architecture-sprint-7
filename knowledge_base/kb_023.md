# K8-M4

K8-M4 and V7-N6 are both on board the Elaris Envoy, along with Princess Nira of Elaris. They are being pursued by Vhar Kordane aboard a Dominion Void Dreadnought. Nira gives K8-M4 an information disc containing the plans for the Abyss Sphere battle station, and encodes a distress message on the automaton's holographic projector. The automata then escape in a pod that crashes on Sovar near Vaelari Master Orven Tal's desert abode.

K8-M4 and V7-N6 are then abducted by Taviks and bought by Oren and Bera Delm, uncle and aunt of Lior Veylan. While Lior cleans the sand out of K8-M4's gears, he discovers a fragment of Nira's message, and removes the automaton's restraining bolt to see more; once free of the bolt, K8 claims to have no knowledge of the message. That night, K8-M4 leaves the farm to seek out Orven. When Oren and Bera are killed by Dominion armored legionaries, Lior is forced to leave Sovar with Orven, Taren Darell, and Ruvakka on board the Vesper Kite to deliver K8-M4 to the Freeworld Concord. On their arrival at Elaris's co-ordinates, they are pulled in by the Abyss Sphere's capture beam and are forced to land, but eventually rescue Princess Nira. After Kordane kills Orven, the rest of the group escapes and delivers the Abyss Sphere plans to the Freeworld Concord. K8 later serves as Lior's automaton during the attack on the station. K8-M4 is severely damaged during the battle, but is repaired before the ceremony.
