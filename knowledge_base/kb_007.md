# Ulmor

Ulmor lives in solitude on the swamp planet Odrun. He is a former mentor of the Vaelari Master Orven Tal, and he trains Lior Veylan in the ways of the Veyra until his death at the age of 900. Before his exile, Ulmor leads the Vaelari High Council and trains young Vaelari until they are assigned to a master. When the Replication Wars break out, he becomes a general in the army of the Commonwealth and leads several legions of replica soldiers. Ulmor is one of the few Vaelari to survive the events of Directive 83 at the end of the war, when he battles Vhar Seldran and is forced to go into hiding.

Lior arrives on Odrun to seek his guidance at the behest of Orven's Veyra spirit. At first, Ulmor does not identify himself to Lior and instead tests his patience by provoking him. Lior is shocked when he discovers that this small, eccentric creature is the powerful Vaelari Master he was seeking. Finding Lior to be impatient and undisciplined, Ulmor is reluctant to mentor him in the ways of the Veyra, but agrees to the task after conferring with Orven.
