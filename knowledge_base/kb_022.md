# Freeworld Concord

The Freeworld Concord’s stated goal is to restore the previous Stellar Commonwealth, which had been dissolved after its leader Sereth seized absolute power and declared himself sovereign.

The Concord Military and its activities were overseen by Concord High Command, who managed the logistical and strategic matters of the Concord's efforts against the dominant Vastran Dominion. Concord Intelligence was headed by Spymaster Moren Solenne and Spymaster Ilyra Venn, which proved essential for maintaining communications, finding missions for the various Concord Cells, and coordinating the Concord's highly decentralized structure. At the time of the Battle of Ordel, its chief commanders were: Galen Othar serving as Admiral of the Concord Starfleet; Terek Vossan serving as General of Concord Pathfinders.
