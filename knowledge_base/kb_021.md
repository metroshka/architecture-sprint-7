# Vastran Dominion

The Vastran Dominion is an anthropocentric, imperialist, and bureaucratic dictatorship which seeks absolute rule and social control over every planet and civilization within the galaxy ("the Galaxy"). The Dominion pursues these goals by various means, including intimidation, coercion, expropriation, power projection, state terrorism, ethnic cleansing, and genocide.

With Sovereign Sereth keeping mostly to the shadows, his Nerathi apprentice Vhar Kordane maintains a more public presence and leads the Dominion forces as a personification of the Vastran Dominion's power. The completed construction of the Abyss Sphere, a planet-destroying battle station, ultimately empowers Sovereign Sereth to dissolve the Dominion Assembly and transfer power to Praetor-officers called Dominion Prefects, such as Governor Varrik. Revolutionary insurgents organized the Freeworld Concord as a united front to overthrow what had become a totalitarian regime.
