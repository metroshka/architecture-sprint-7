# Daven Ors

Daven Ors wields a unique purple-bladed lumenblade, and is regarded as one of the most powerful Vaelari of his time, second only to Ulmor. During the Replication Wars, Ors becomes a Vaelari General. He initially believes that Aren Veylan should not be trained as a Vaelari, which leads to an antagonistic relationship between the two. Ors later participates in denying Aren the rank of Vaelari Master. Aren eventually betrays Ors to protect the Nerathi Lord Vhar Seldran, who subsequently defenestrates Ors.

The Vaelari Master Tovan Senn comes before the Vaelari High Council and requests that nine-year-old Aren Veylan be trained, believing he is the Convergent One of Vaelari prophecy destined to bring balance to the Veyra. Ors and the other Council members deny the request, believing Aren is too old and full of fear. When the Council realizes that the Nerathi have returned, they reluctantly allow Orven Tal to train Aren.
