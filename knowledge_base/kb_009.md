# Selya Mereth

Selya Mereth is the recently-elected fourteen-year-old queen of Nerava. She is dedicated to ending her planet's occupation by the Mercantile Union. She negotiates with Union Viceroy Vorun Tek, who attempts to veyra her to sign a treaty which would legally allow the Union's occupation. Selya escapes from Nerava with the help of Vaelari Master Tovan Senn and his Novice apprentice Orven Tal. On the way to Velisar, they are forced to land on Sovar to repair their ship. Disguised as a handmaiden, Selya meets the nine-year-old slave boy Aren Veylan. She witnesses him win a skimmer race that both aids her mission to Velisar and secures his freedom. She and Aren bond as they journey from Sovar to Velisar.

Selya consults with Delegate Sereth, who encourages her to appeal to the Assembly to resolve Nerava's conflict with the Union. Sereth, who is secretly the Nerathi Lord Vhar Seldran, persuades her to make a motion to have Supreme Chancellor Orelis removed from office, which enables Sereth to be elected as his successor. Selya returns to Nerava to fight for her planet's freedom, enlisting the aid of Zal Vesh and the Neral people. The Nerals create a diversion to lure the Union's automaton armies away from Sereva; which has been seized by Tek. A small veyra led by Selya enters the palace and captures Tek, which ends the occupation of Nerava.
