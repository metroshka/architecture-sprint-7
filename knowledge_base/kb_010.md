# Tovan Senn

Tovan and his Vaelari apprentice Orven Tal are sent to Nerava to resolve a conflict involving the Mercantile Union, a business conglomerate that has blockaded the planet for political leverage. When the two Vaelari arrive, the Union attempts to assassinate them. Tovan and Orven retreat to Nerava, rescue its besieged queen, Selya Mereth, then leave for Velisar, the galactic capital. During the journey, they land on Sovar to repair their ship. Tovan encounters a nine-year-old slave boy named Aren Veylan, whose potential with the Veyra is the highest ever detected. The Vaelari Master becomes intrigued when Aren's mother tells him that the boy had no father. Believing that Aren could be the "Convergent One" of Vaelari prophecy destined to bring balance to the Veyra, Tovan secures the boy's freedom and decides to bring him before the Vaelari Council on Velisar. Before leaving Sovar, Tovan duels with a mysterious warrior dressed in black.

On Velisar, Tovan asks the Council to allow Aren to be trained as a Vaelari. Master Ulmor senses fear in the boy, and the Council denies the request. Undaunted, Tovan vows that he will train Aren himself. Selya, Tovan and Orven return to Nerava, where they once again encounter the dark warrior, who reveals himself to be the Nerathi Lord Vhar Zherak. During a ferocious lumenblade battle, Zherak fatally wounds Tovan. After defeating Zherak, Orven promises to train Aren for Tovan before he dies.
