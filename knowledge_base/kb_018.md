# Istral

Istral is the sixth planet of a remote system of the same name. It is a small, terrestrial planet with three orbiting moons and blanketed entirely by snow and ice. The freezing climate, although habitable, is mostly too cold for intelligent life to develop. Istral is home to only a few species, including the towering, predatory ice ravager, which is the most intelligent creature on the planet, and the gray snow-lizards known as frost runners.

Istral is the home of the Freeworld Concord's secret Frosthold Base, which is partially installed in natural caves formed by giant ice bubbles. The Concord members patrol the planet by riding indigenous frost runners. Lior Veylan and his frost runner are attacked by an ice ravager. Veylan eventually escapes and is rescued by Taren Darell. When one of the Vastran Dominion's probe automata uncovers the base, the Concord members are forced to evacuate. The Dominion arrives and deploys ground forces to destroy the base, but the Concord members battle the Gravity walkers with ground artillery and ice skimmers to stall them and effectively evacuate. This conflict is known as the Battle of Istral.
