# Taren Darell

Taren Darell and his Ruunak co-pilot Ruvakka accept a charter to transport Lior Veylan and Orven Tal from Sovar to Elaris on their ship, the Vesper Kite. Before they depart, Darell is cornered by the bounty hunter Heskor, who threatens him. Darell shoots and kills Heskor, then boards the Kite with his passengers. They are attacked by Dominion armored legionaries, but elude them by accelerating to light speed. When they arrive at their destination, they discover that Elaris has been obliterated by the Abyss Sphere, the Dominion's colossal battle station. The Kite is captured and brought aboard the Abyss Sphere, but Darell and his companions avoid detection and infiltrate the station. When they discover that Princess Nira is being held captive, Lior convinces Darell and Ruvakka to help him rescue her by promising Darell a large reward. After delivering Lior and Nira to the Freeworld Concord, Darell collects his payment and prepares to leave. Lior asks him to aid the Concord members in their assault on the Abyss Sphere, but Darell refuses. He returns unexpectedly during the battle, saving Lior's life and allowing him to destroy the Abyss Sphere. Afterwards, Nira presents Darell and Lior with medals for their heroism.

Taren Darell is still with the Freeworld Concord, serving at the headquarters on the ice planet Istral. He intends to leave to pay off his debt to Borvo the Ghoran, but delays his departure when Lior goes missing. Darell rides alone into freezing temperatures and finds Lior nearly dead from exposure. He builds a shelter and they survive until they are rescued. Later, Darell and Ruvakka destroy a Dominion probe automaton, but not before the Dominion is alerted to the location of the Concord base. Darell, Ruvakka and Nira escape in the Vesper Kite and fly into an asteroid field. While repairing the ship, Darell and Nira share a kiss. The group then seeks aid from Darell's friend Velin Ardos in Aerial Haven. Under duress by the Dominion, Velin betrays them to it, and Kordane tortures Darell as part of his plan to trap Lior. Kordane freezes Darell in cryostone and gives him to the bounty hunter Boren Vesk, who plans to deliver him to Borvo. Before Darell is put into cryostone hibernation, Nira confesses her love for him.
