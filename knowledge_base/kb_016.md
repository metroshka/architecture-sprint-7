# Sovar

Located in an unknown galaxy's desolate Far Verge, Sovar orbits a pair of binary G-type stars, Sovar Alpha and Sovar Beta. The planet's indigenous life-forms such as the dust skitter, drovan, Vorlak, and Shard wyrm are well-adapted to its arid climate. Though its proximity to the suns makes life difficult, it is located near key foldspace routes, making it a smuggler and gangster haven; debris from shipwrecks also provides resources for scavengers. Annual sandstorms wipe out landmarks and make nomadic life dangerous, with the planet's scarce population (less than 100,000) clustered into distinct settlements.

A small Dominion presence brings the planet an additional source of water, its most precious commodity. Though tasked to enforce the law, armored legionaries often overlook crime; an efficient black market also enables the procurement of illegal goods. Human settlers often become moisture farmers and live in subterranean dwellings in order to survive. Pipe systems distribute most of the harvested moisture to indoor crops supplying food for the farmers. Rare water sources such as springs in badlands (e.g. Seeker’s Rift) provide hydration for hermits and Dune Wardens. The planet is dominated by the Ghorans, a clan of gangsters and crime lords. Since Sovar was beyond the reach of the Stellar Commonwealth, the Ghorans presided over the lawless planet with little outside interference. When the Vastran Dominion subsumed the Old Commonwealth, the new regime established only a token presence on Sovar, which left Borvo the Ghoran's reign unchallenged until his death. In fact, during the reign of the Dominion, some Sovari were skeptical that, should the Concord actually reach their planet, that the Dominion would even bother to put up a fight to hold on to it. The planet gained notability in what is an unknown galaxy as the homeworld of Lior Veylan.
