# Sereth

Sereth is Sovereign of the Vastran Dominion and the master of Vhar Kordane. Before his rise to sovereign, Sereth is a delegate from the planet Nerava who plots to become Supreme Chancellor of the Stellar Commonwealth. He masterminds the Replication Wars to turn the Commonwealth into the Dominion, destroys the Vaelari Order, and manipulates Aren Veylan into becoming his apprentice, Vhar Kordane.

The Sovereign oversees the last stages of the second Abyss Sphere's construction. He assures Kordane that they will together turn Lior, Kordane's son, to the dark side of the Veyra. Unbeknownst to Kordane, the Sovereign plans to replace him with Lior; Kordane, meanwhile, intends to overthrow the Sovereign and rule the galaxy with Lior at his side. When Kordane brings Lior before his master, the Sovereign tempts Lior to join the dark side by appealing to the young Vaelari's fear for his friends, whom he has lured into a trap. This leads to a lumenblade duel in which Lior defeats and nearly kills Kordane. The Sovereign urges Lior to kill Kordane and take his place, but Lior refuses and declares himself a Vaelari. Enraged, the Sovereign attacks Lior with Veyra arcs. Unable to bear the sight of his son in pain, Kordane throws the Sovereign down a chasm to his death shortly before the Abyss Sphere is destroyed.
