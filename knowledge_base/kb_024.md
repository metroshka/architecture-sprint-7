# V7-N6

V7-N6 is a protocol automaton designed to assist in etiquette and translation, and is fluent in over six million forms of communication.

V7-N6 was built on Sovar by the nine-year-old slave boy Aren Veylan. The protocol automaton meets his future companion, K8-M4, along with Vaelari Master Tovan Senn, Queen Selya Mereth of Nerava and the Neral Zal Vesh. V7-N6 and K8-M4 help improve Aren's skimmer racer for the Velora Circuit race, and V7-N6 becomes part of Aren's pit crew. V7-N6 and Aren part ways when Tovan frees the boy from slavery and takes him off-planet. Before leaving, Aren assures the automaton that his mother Senra will not sell him.
