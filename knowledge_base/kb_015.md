# Ilyra Venn

Ilyra Venn is a novice-commander of the 703rd Legion in the Grand Army of the Commonwealth and continues to learn the ways of the Vaelari as Aren's apprentice. The two develop a mutual fondness, at times taking great risks to protect or save one another. Some of Aren's actions taken out of concern for Ilyra expose his darker tendencies, such as his torture of prisoners who may know her location when she goes missing. Ilyra also finds mentorship from Captain Varn, a replica soldier with whom she and Aren serve throughout the war. Ilyra is framed and imprisoned for a deadly explosion and a subsequent murder, both of which were committed by her friend Kelira Den. Although eventually exonerated, she becomes disillusioned with the Vaelari Council and departs from the Vaelari Order.

Before departing to Rethar, Varn is promoted to Commander in the 418th Division (split from the 703rd Legion) with Ilyra as an advisor to capture the former Nerathi Lord Zherak. During the siege of Rethar, Ilyra confronts Zherak; he reveals that Seldran intends to make Aren his new apprentice and offers to join forces to prevent this, but she does not believe him. Ilyra defeats and captures Zherak, but on the way to Velisar, she senses her former master in trouble, though unaware that he has fallen to the dark side. When Directive 83 is issued, Ilyra's replica soldiers, including Varn, turn on her, but she escapes and manages to remove Varn's chip controlling his brain, while also freeing Zherak to create a distraction. Ilyra and Varn escape the Peregrin-class Void Dreadnought they are on before it crashes on a small moon, where they bury all the deceased replica soldiers. Ilyra also discards one of her lumenblades before parting ways with Varn.
