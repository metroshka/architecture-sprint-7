# Velin Ardos

Velin Ardos is an old friend of Taren Darell. He is the previous owner of Taren's ship, the Vesper Kite, and he is the administrator of Aerial Haven. When Taren, Princess Nira, Ruvakka, and V7-N6 arrive at Aerial Haven, Velin welcomes them as guests, but soon betrays them to Vhar Kordane, who plans to use the group as bait to ensnare Lior Veylan. Velin reveals later that he had reluctantly agreed to betray them after Kordane threatened to put Aerial Haven under Dominion control if he refused. Velin allows Kordane to freeze Taren in cryostone, but when Kordane takes Nira and Ruvakka prisoner, his conscience gets the better of him. In the ensuing evacuation of Aerial Haven, Velin helps the group escape in the Kite. Soon after, he assists in rescuing Lior from the underside of Aerial Haven. Afterwards, he promises to help find Taren.

Velin helps Lior rescue Taren from the crime lord Borvo the Ghoran. Later, Velin is made a general in the Freeworld Concord, and pilots the Vesper Kite in an attack on the second Abyss Sphere.
