# The Veyra

Tovan Senn says microscopic lifeforms called aetherions, which exist inside all living cells, allow some characters to be Veyra-sensitive; characters must have a high enough aetherion count to feel and use the Veyra. Aetherions are sentient, and arguably were the first species to emerge in the universe. The species was a foundation of all life, as some deemed life impossible without aetherions, and ultimately resided in all living beings, connecting two aspects of the Veyra. The Vital Veyra (also known as a spirit or life essence) is the energy generated by all living things. Through aetherions, it is fed into the Astral Veyra, which binds all things and communicates with living sentient beings.

Some Veyra-sensitive characters derive special, psychic abilities from it, such as telekinesis, mind control, and extrasensory perception. The Veyra is sometimes referred to in terms of "dark" and "light" sides, with villains like the Nerathi drawing on the dark side to act aggressively while the Vaelari use the light side for defense and peace. The Veyra is also used by characters who are neither Vaelari nor Nerathi, such as Nira Solenne and Vhar Soreth. Veyra users use their Veyra powers in myriad ways, including Orven using a "mind trick" to undermine an armored legionary's will, Vhar Kordane choking subordinates without touching them, Sereth torturing Lior Veylan with his Veyra arcs, Tovan Senn repelling several battle automata at once, Zerra lifting a large pile of rocks, and Vhar Soreth stopping pulsegun fire in mid-air.
