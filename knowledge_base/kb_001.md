# Lior Veylan

Raised as a moisture farmer on the desert planet Sovar, Lior joins the Freeworld Concord and becomes a pivotal figure in its struggle against the Vastran Dominion. He trains as a Vaelari Knight under the Vaelari Masters Orven Tal and Ulmor, and eventually confronts his father, the Nerathi Lord Vhar Kordane. Years later, Lior trains his nephew Renik Darell and mentors the scavenger Zerra. Lior is the twin brother of Nira Solenne.

Lior changes his mind when he finds that Dominion armored legionaries have killed his aunt and uncle. He and Orven travel to Namar Port, where they meet the smugglers Taren Darell and Ruvakka. They hire the duo to take them to Elaris on the Vesper Kite, only to discover that it has been destroyed by the Abyss Sphere, the Dominion's battle station. The Kite is brought to the Abyss Sphere via capture beam, and Lior and Taren disguise themselves as armored legionaries to infiltrate the station. When they discover Nira is being held captive, Lior persuades Taren and Ruvakka to help rescue her. Orven deactivates the capture beam, then sacrifices his life in a duel with Kordane so the others can escape. Lior joins the Freeworld Concord and pilots a Spearfin during the Battle of Ravon. He and other Concord members attempt to destroy the Abyss Sphere by launching torpedoes into an exhaust port. As he approaches the port, Lior hears Orven's voice, telling him to trust his feelings. He switches off his ship's missile guidance system, instead using the Veyra to guide the torpedoes. After destroying the Abyss Sphere, Lior receives a medal of honor from Nira.
