# Lumenblade

The lumenblade is the signature weapon of both the light side-wielding Vaelari Order and the dark side-wielding Nerathi Order, though it can be used as an ordinary weapon or tool by any individual, regardless of their Veyra sensitivity. The Vaelari use lumenblades of various colors, typically blue, green and—to a lesser extent—yellow, orange and purple, while the Nerathi wield red-bladed lumenblades to distinguish themselves from the Vaelari. The color of a lumenblade's blade is given by its oriven crystal, which powers the blade and whose color is determined by the user's connection to the Veyra. A lumenblade's hilt is built by its wielder and is, therefore, unique in design. There are several variations outside of the traditional single-bladed lumenblade, such as the double-bladed lumenblade (most famously wielded by Vhar Zherak), the curved-hilted lumenblade (wielded by Count Nemeris and Aserra Velith), the crossguard lumenblade (used by Vhar Soreth and occasionally by Vaelari), and the Umbral Edge, forged by the Rethari Vaelari Tarel Nys and primarily wielded by the Veyra-insensitive rulers of the planet Rethar (including Prel Nys and Dara Arvek).

The source of a lumenblade's power is an oriven crystal. These crystals are also the power source of the Abyss Sphere's annihilation beam.
