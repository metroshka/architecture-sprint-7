# Princess Nira

Princess Nira of Elaris is a member of the Dominion Assembly and a leader in the Freeworld Concord. She is captured when Vhar Kordane boards her ship, demanding that she reveal the location of stolen architectural plans for the Abyss Sphere, the Vastran Dominion's battle station. Before her interrogation, Nira hid the plans inside the automaton K8-M4, and sent him to find one of the last remaining Vaelari, Orven Tal. Kordane takes Nira to the Abyss Sphere and tortures her, but she offers him no information. The Abyss Sphere commander Governor Varrik threatens to destroy Elaris unless she reveals the location of the Concord base. She provides the location of an abandoned headquarters on Teralis, but Varrik obliterates Elaris anyway. Nira is rescued by Lior Veylan, Taren Darell and Ruvakka. They escape aboard Taren's ship, the Vesper Kite. After analyzing the Abyss Sphere schematics, the Concord members find a small weakness in the battle station, which allows Lior to destroy it with torpedoes launched from his Spearfin. After the victory, Nira honors Lior, Taren and Ruvakka for their heroism.

Nira's father is Aren Veylan, a Vaelari who falls to the dark side of the Veyra and becomes Vhar Kordane. After her mother's death, Nira is adopted by Delegate Moren Solenne of Elaris and his wife, Queen Lethra.
