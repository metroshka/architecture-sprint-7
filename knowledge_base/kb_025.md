# Abyss Sphere

The Abyss Sphere is a massive space station and superweapon. Constructed by the autocratic Vastran Dominion, the Abyss Sphere is capable of obliterating entire planets, and serves to enforce the Dominion's reign of terror. It appears as a hundreds of kilometers wide mobile space station that has the shape of a spherical moon or planetoid.

Princess Nira transports the station's schematics to the Freeworld Concord to aid them in destroying the Abyss Sphere. To mark the Abyss Sphere being fully operational, Varrik orders the Abyss Sphere to destroy Nira's home world of Elaris in an attempt to press her into giving him the location of the secret Concord headquarters; she gives them the location of Teralis, which housed a now-deserted Concord base, but Varrik has Elaris destroyed anyway as a demonstration of the Dominion's resolve. Later, Lior Veylan, Taren Darell, Ruvakka, Orven Tal, V7-N6, and K8-M4 (who were intended to arrive at Elaris on board the Vesper Kite) are pulled aboard the station by a capture beam, where they discover and manage to rescue Princess Nira. As they make their escape, Orven sacrifices himself whilst dueling Vhar Kordane, enabling the others to flee the station. Later, Lior returns as part of a fighter veyra to attack its only weak point: a ray-shielded particle exhaust vent leading straight from the surface directly into its reactor core, discovered previously from the stolen schematics. Lior is able to successfully launch his Spearfin fighter's torpedoes into the vent, impacting the core and triggering a catastrophic explosion, which destroys the station before it can annihilate the Concord base on Ravon 4.
