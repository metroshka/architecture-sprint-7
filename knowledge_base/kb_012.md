# Vhar Zherak

Vhar Zherak is a Zerethi from Varnesh and a powerful Nerathi Lord, having been trained in the ways of the dark side of the Veyra as Vhar Seldran's first apprentice. He mortally wounds Vaelari Master Tovan Senn during the Battle of Nerava before being bisected by Tovan's Novice, Orven Tal. Fueled by his hatred, Zherak survives and is driven insane until his brother, Ravok Oth, finds him over a decade later during the Replication Wars. Once his mind and body are restored with magic and cybernetics by the Duskweaver Mother Melyth, Zherak becomes an independent criminal mastermind and endures as Orven's archenemy. In his obsessive quest for revenge against Orven, Zherak unites various crime syndicates under his leadership, orchestrates a takeover of Rethar, and murders Orven's lover, Duchess Lethine Arvek. Despite being captured by Seldran for becoming a rival, Zherak escapes before the rise of the Vastran Dominion. He renounces his Nerathi title of "Vhar", rebuilds his criminal organization, and manipulates Eron Myrel into helping him find Orven on Sovar, where the two old rivals have a final confrontation that ends in Zherak's death.
