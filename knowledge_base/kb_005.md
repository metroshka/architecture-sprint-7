# Ruvakka

Ruvakka is a Ruunak—a tall, hairy, highly intelligent species originating from the planet Kharuun. He is 7.5 feet (2.3 m) tall, and typically wears only a bandolier and a tool pouch. He carries a coilcaster, a traditional Ruunak weapon, and he speaks the Ruunak language Ruunaki. He is the loyal friend of the smuggler Taren Darell. Ruvakka is also the co-pilot of Taren's starship, the Vesper Kite.

Ruvakka and Taren Darell are smugglers who accept a charter to transport Lior Veylan and Orven Tal to the planet Elaris aboard their ship, the Vesper Kite. When they find the planet obliterated by the Abyss Sphere, Ruvakka and Taren help their passengers rescue Princess Nira and take her to the Concord base on Ravon 4. During the battle, Ruvakka and Taren save Lior from being killed by Vhar Kordane, which allows Lior to destroy the Abyss Sphere. After the battle, Nira acknowledges Ruvakka for his achievements alongside Lior and Taren.
