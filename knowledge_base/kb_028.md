# Spearfin fighter

The M-42C Spearfin is produced by the Orvatek Corporation, which had previously supplied the SRA-260 and K-72 Seeker starfighters to the Stellar Commonwealth during the Replication Wars. When the Vastran Dominion ordered them to produce a new starfighter for the Dominion Navy, Orvatek engineers took inspiration from their previous work to create the Spearfin. Production was already underway when politicking resulted in the Dominion choosing instead to go with Dromar Systems' VEX fighter. Faced with an overproduction of expensive starfighters, a new market was found when agents of the Freeworld Concord contacted Orvatek about acquiring their stock of Spearfins, to which Orvatek eagerly agreed. The Spearfin quickly became a symbol of the Concord thanks to its use in several spectacular victories and the role it played in ultimately defeating the Dominion. After the Dominion's defeat, the Spearfin starfighters became a staple of the New Commonwealth fleet and later the Dawn Coalition, with Orvatek-Neris supplying upgraded M-48 and M-61 models.

Compared to the VEX fighter, the Spearfin is slower and less maneuverable in space, but boasts superior firepower, defenses, atmospheric maneuverability, and a supraluminal fold drive. Its four laser cannons can be fired singly, in pairs or all at once, and each launcher can carry up to three proton torpedoes. In addition to deflector shields, the Spearfin possesses a titanium alloy armored hull, electronic countermeasures, and armored cockpit. An inertial dampener protects the pilot from high-g maneuvers, while an anti-gravity "grav-lift" provides for flight in planetary atmospheres.

Instead of a dedicated navigational system, the Spearfin uses a navtech automaton (such as K8-M4) that fits in a socket behind the cockpit. The automaton can hold up to 10 foldspace coordinates, make minor repairs to the craft, and assume full control of the Spearfin if needed. As with the pilot's ejection seat, an explosive charge can eject the automaton from the Spearfin in an emergency.
