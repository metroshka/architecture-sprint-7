# Boren Vesk

Boren Vesk is one of six bounty hunters assembled by Vhar Kordane, who promises a reward to whoever captures the crew of the Vesper Kite. Vesk tracks the starship to Aerial Haven, where Kordane captures its passengers and tortures its captain, Taren Darell. Aiming to collect the bounty Borvo the Ghoran has placed on Darell, Vesk questions Kordane regarding the cryostone freezing, which Kordane intends to use on his true target, Lior Veylan. Kordane promises that the Dominion will compensate Vesk if Darell dies, but the smuggler survives and Kordane turns him over to Vesk, who leaves to deliver him to Borvo in his ship, the Night Pursuer.

Boren Vesk is at Borvo's palace on Sovar, where Taren Darell's friends are captured while trying to save him. Vesk aims his weapon at Princess Nira (disguised as bounty hunter Huvren) when she threatens Borvo with a thermal detonator, and he later travels on Borvo's sail barge to the Nythar Chasm (home of a sessile carnivorous creature known as a vorlak) to witness the prisoners' execution. When the prisoners revolt and fight back, Vesk flies over and briefly fights Lior. Ruvakka later warns Darell, who is still blind after being frozen in cryostone, that Vesk is near. As Darell turns around, he inadvertently hits Vesk's rocket pack, setting it off and propelling Vesk into the side of the sail barge, from which he falls into the vorlak's mouth.
